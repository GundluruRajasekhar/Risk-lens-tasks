RiskLens requirements

Risk and regulatory copilot for Banking and NBFC teams. Version 0.1 (prototype).

1. Problem

Fraud, liquidity, credit risk and regulatory reporting (AML, Basel, local rules) are largely manual. Analysts pull data from many systems, build reports by hand and struggle to show auditors how a number was produced.

2. Users

UserMain need
Fraud analystSee and triage suspicious transactions fast
AML / compliance officerDraft STRs and regulatory returns with evidence
Treasury / ALMMonitor LCR, NSFR and liquidity gaps
Credit risk managerTrack NPA, staging and ECL by segment
Internal auditorVerify any output: inputs, rules, time and author

3. Functional requirements

IDRequirementPriority
FR-1Accept natural language questions and map them to a risk domain (fraud, AML, liquidity, credit, capital).Must
FR-2Show a live signal feed ranked by severity, each opening the underlying answer.Must
FR-3Score transactions with named rules and explain why each was flagged.Must
FR-4Draft STR narratives for analyst review. Never file automatically.Must
FR-5Calculate LCR, NSFR, CRAR, CET1 and leverage against regulatory minimums.Must
FR-6Report GNPA, Stage 2 and ECL cover by loan segment.Should
FR-7Attach an audit record to every output: ID, timestamp, question, rules, data source, user, SHA-256 hash.Must
FR-8Export audit record as JSON and print the report.Must
FR-9Replace keyword matching with an LLM that only calls approved data tools.Should
FR-10Role-based access and maker-checker approval before any regulatory submission.Must (production)

4. Non-functional requirements

AreaRequirement
AuditabilityEvery number traces to a source, rule version and timestamp. Records are tamper-evident.
AccuracyFigures come from deterministic calculations, never generated by the model.
LatencyAnswers in under 3 seconds on live data.
SecurityEncrypt data, mask PII in prompts, keep logs immutable.
PrivacyData stays inside the institution's environment.
AccessibilityKeyboard navigable, readable on mobile.

5. Regulatory scope

AML (PMLA, FIU-IND STR), Basel III (capital, LCR, NSFR), RBI norms for asset classification and provisioning, Ind AS 109 staging. Thresholds in the prototype are illustrative and must be confirmed with compliance before use.

6. Out of scope for prototype

Live core banking integration, real filing to regulators, model training, real customer data.

7. Success measures

Time to draft an STR cut by half, every output reproducible by an auditor, false-positive rate tracked per rule.
